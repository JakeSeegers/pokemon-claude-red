# Ideas for later

The study cases are focused on one goal for now: learning the diagnoses. These ideas came up while tuning them. They
are good directions for later, or for special cases, but they would make the game more complicated today.

## Counseling skills
- **Reflections and summaries as question types.** OARS: open questions, affirmations, reflections, summaries. A good
  reflection ("It sounds like the nights are the hardest part") could earn PATIENCE and make the patient say more.
- **Safety screening.** Some cases have risk (for example, a patient who says their family would be better off without
  them). The game could grade whether you ask about suicide directly. Asking does not plant the idea.
- **Intake coverage.** The case notes could list which intake areas you covered: medical history, substances, family,
  social life, safety.

## Outcomes besides a diagnosis
- **Referral:** "see a doctor now" (the heart-attack case), "see a specialist", or "treat the cause, then reassess".
- **Provisional diagnosis:** "rule out X" or "provisional" as a final answer.

## Scope and ethics
- Recognize red flags and refer, instead of diagnosing everything.
- Consent before talking to family; cultural humility; supervision for hard cases.
